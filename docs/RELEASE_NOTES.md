Social to Mealie support.

- New optional setting: the URL of the Social to Mealie add-on. On Home Assistant OS it is detected and filled in when the add-on is running, and it is checked when you save.
- When it is set, **Add to Mealie** on YouTube results goes through the add-on (download, transcription and AI), and the panel shows **Add from link** for Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube and other links.
- Ingredients of recipes created by the add-on are linked to your existing Mealie foods and units, like website imports.
- The Social to Mealie add-on is no longer mistaken for Mealie when detecting add-ons.
- Social to Mealie alone is enough to set up the integration; the search bar is hidden when no search provider is configured.
